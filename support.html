Getting started

How do I set up my subjects?

On first launch, or later from Settings, pick your six subjects and set HL or SL for each. Inside a subject, Create Unit lets you group syllabus points the way your school teaches them, and Exam countdown records upcoming tests with the topics they cover.

How do I practise one concept?

Learn › open the subject › type in the search field at the top. Each result offers Lesson, Questions, MCQ and Cards for that exact syllabus point.

How do I save a question?

Tap the bookmark on any question card while practising or in the past-paper view. Choose Unsorted or a folder, or create one on the spot. Your library is under Learn › subject › Saved questions, where you can sort by marks, type or topic and practise a whole folder.

Tutor and marking

The tutor says AI is off

The AI tutor and AI marking are free and run through Syllabo's server, so they need a connection and a free online account (a new install gets its first day of AI as a guest). Sign in from the Tutor tab, and check that AI is on in Settings › AI marking and tutor. Without AI, IB written answers are still marked on your device against the mark scheme. AP® written answers are not: you tick the points you earned against the scoring guidelines, or sign in to have them marked.

Where are real past papers?

The app ships original IB-format questions. Real papers are IBO copyright and are not bundled, and real AP® exams are College Board copyright, so the AP® questions are original too. If your school gives you access, type or paste their questions in under Learn › subject › Saved questions › Import, and tag each one with its syllabus points and marks.

AP® courses (beta)

How do I study an AP® course?

Choose AP at the start of setup, then pick your courses. The AP® page lists the 12 courses. Courses are in beta, so they can change, and marks and scores are estimates.

What does the 1 to 5 mean?

It is an estimate from typical cut scores, not an official AP® score. A full practice exam gives one when every answer is marked. College Board sets the real scores.

Why am I marking my own answer?

In AP® Biology and AP® Psychology you mark written answers yourself for now, because AI marking did not pass our accuracy check for those courses. In other courses this happens when AI marking is not available, for example offline or as a guest without AI. Multiple choice and number answers are marked for you either way.

I picked the wrong curriculum

After setup you only see your own curriculum. Go to Settings › Help and feedback › Picked the wrong curriculum? and email us from there. We will reply with a code that opens the switch.

Privacy

Can I turn off usage counts?

Yes: Settings › Privacy › Share anonymous usage counts. The privacy policy lists what they contain.

Accounts and friends

I forgot my password

On the sign-in screen tap Forgot your password? Online accounts get a 6-digit code by email; enter it with your new password. Accounts kept only on this phone are reset with Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode.

Friends cannot find me

Check Friends › Discoverable by username is on, and share your exact @username. With online accounts, friends can be on any phone; both of you need an online account.

How do I delete my account?

Home › your initials (the round button, top left) › Delete account. This deletes the account and its study data from this device straight away and, for an online account, deletes it online too. It cannot be undone. Deleting your account below has the full details, including how to ask without the app.

Deleting your account

You can delete your Syllabo account, made by Tyler Cho, at any time, from the app or by email.

In the app (iPhone, iPad, Mac and Android): open Home, tap your initials (the round button, top left), then tap Delete account and confirm. The account is deleted straight away.

Without the app: email syllabosupport@gmail.com from the email address on your account, with the subject "Delete my account" and your username if you know it. We delete the account and reply to confirm within 7 days. If you write from a different address, we may ask you to confirm the account is yours first.

What is deleted:

  • Your online account: name, email address, username and sign-in details
  • The backup of your study data kept with the account: subjects, progress, buddy, preferences and saved questions
  • Your friends, friend requests, blocks and study pacts, and your place on leaderboards
  • Collectibles tied to your account
  • The account and its study data on the device you delete it from
  • If you used Sign in with Apple, Syllabo's access to your Apple ID is removed

What is kept, and for how long:

  • Custom leagues you made pass to the next member; a league with no one left is deleted
  • A duel from the last 7 days in which the other player has coins at stake stays until it is settled for them, and is removed within 30 days
  • Feedback you sent is no longer linked to your account; it is kept until it is dealt with, and for at most two years
  • A report another player made about you keeps the name and username shown at the time, so we can finish dealing with it
  • Anonymous usage counts never identified you, so there is nothing in them to delete

Guest accounts are kept only on your device and never reach our server. Deleting the app removes them.
