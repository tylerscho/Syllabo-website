IB Maths AA revision for HL and SL

Practise mathematics: analysis and approaches with a tangent problem, a logarithm equation and worked marking points.

What to revise

Maths AA covers algebra, functions, geometry and trigonometry, probability and statistics, and calculus. Choose a few questions each week to solve without a calculator. Write the intermediate steps and check any restrictions on the original expression before accepting an answer.

Practice questions

Tangent to a curve · 3 marks

Find the equation of the tangent to f(x) = x3 − 2x at x = 2.

Show marking points
  1. Differentiate to get f′(x) = 3x2 − 2.
  2. At x = 2 the gradient is 10 and the point is (2, 4).
  3. The tangent is y − 4 = 10(x − 2), or y = 10x − 16.

Logarithms and domain · 4 marks

Solve log2(x − 1) + log2(x + 1) = 3.

Show marking points
  1. Both logarithm arguments must be positive, so x > 1.
  2. Combine the logarithms to get log2(x2 − 1) = 3.
  3. Convert to x2 − 1 = 8, giving candidates x = 3 and x = −3.
  4. Only x = 3 is in the original domain.

Two mistakes to check

  • Evaluate the derivative for the tangent gradient and the original function for the point. They answer different questions.
  • Check the domain before combining logarithms. A positive product can hide two negative factors.
