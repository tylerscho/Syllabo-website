IB command terms and how to answer them

See what to write when a question asks you to state, describe, explain, calculate, compare, contrast, discuss, evaluate or justify.

What the question is asking for

Read the command term with the mark allocation and the rest of the prompt. The same topic can need a trend, a cause or a judgement depending on the wording.

State
Give the fact directly. For a question about the gas taken up during photosynthesis, write “carbon dioxide”.
Describe
Say what happens. If the graph rises and then levels off, describe both parts and use values if they help specify the trend.
Explain
Give the cause and connect it to the outcome. In the potato prompt, connect the water potential difference to net water uptake and the increase in mass.
Calculate
Show the main steps and give the answer, including units where relevant. For elasticity, write the two percentage changes before taking their ratio.
Compare
Pair similarities in the same sentence or row. For example, both a carbon tax and an emissions trading scheme can raise the cost of emitting carbon.
Contrast
Pair differences using the same basis. A carbon tax fixes a price per unit of emissions, whereas a cap sets the total quantity allowed under a cap-and-trade scheme.
Discuss
Build a considered argument with reasons, evidence and limitations. For a subsidy, weigh who gains from lower prices against who bears the cost and what the outcome depends on.
Evaluate
Weigh strengths and weaknesses against a criterion, and reach a supported judgement. For a paper on policy, make clear whether you are judging equity, cost or impact.
Justify
Support your choice or conclusion with evidence or reasoning. If you choose a model for data, point to a feature of the data that supports that model.

Apply the command before opening the answer

Try the Economics subsidy question or the Maths AA logarithms question. Before looking at the solution, underline the command term and write what kind of answer it calls for. Use your subject guide for course-specific definitions.
