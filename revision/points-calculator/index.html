How the total works

Each of your six subjects contributes 1 to 7 points, at either HL or SL. TOK and the extended essay jointly add 0 to 3 points. For example, six grades of 6 give 36 subject points. A B in TOK and a C in the extended essay add 2, for 38 out of 45.

Points, predicted grades and diploma eligibility

Your school supplies the predicted grades. Use those grades here to calculate their total. Raw marks and grade boundaries depend on the course and exam session, so enter the 1 to 7 grades your school gives you. A Diploma also has subject-grade conditions, HL and SL minimums and CAS requirements. An E in either core component is a failing condition.
