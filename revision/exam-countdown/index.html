IB exam countdown for May 2027

Count the days until your first May 2027 IB exam using the date on your school timetable. The same tool works for mocks and later exam sessions.

Check the date with your school

The May session can include papers in April. Enter your own first-paper date from the timetable confirmed by your school coordinator. See the official exam schedule page for published timetables.

Plan backwards from your first paper

Use the first practice session to find two or three weak topics. Revisit them during the week and keep a list of mistakes to redo. Leave time for at least one timed practice session before your first paper.
